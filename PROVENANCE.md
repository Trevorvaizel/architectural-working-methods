# Provenance and portable-edition changes

This collection is curated from Trevor Richard’s local architectural workflow material. It contains working instructions and project-derived corrections, not third-party PDF-production, typography or general frontend skills used to assemble the portfolio. 

## Architectural render credibility

The local method was explicitly revised after Pin-Point render studies in July 2026. Its anti-slop vocabulary follows the local *Anti-AI-Render-Slop Manifesto*, a research-informed synthesis whose research spine includes perspective/shadow research, DOCCI and architectural visualisation practice. The method combines research-informed principles with project-derived review rules.

Portable changes: an unbundled `precise-object-edit` invocation is expressed as a provider-neutral local-edit requirement. The final review reference’s shorthand for targeted correction now repeats the source-controller/keyline condition already in the main method. Required references and optional host display metadata are retained. No client assets or chat excerpts are included.

## Architectural cinematic maquette
