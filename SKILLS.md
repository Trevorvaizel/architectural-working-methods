# Selected methods

## Architectural render credibility

**Task:** Develop a still image or coordinated view set without losing the architectural subject.

**Operation:** Assign geometry, material and mood sources distinct roles; define protected decisions and visible-use inventory; direct light and material response; review the result against the source.

**Project evidence:** Pin-Point render studies in July 2026 led to retained rules for source authority, occupation, planting and revision control.

**Status:** Project-developed method for source-based inspection and correction.

**Package:** [SKILL.md](skills/architectural-render-credibility-v1/SKILL.md), its three reference files and optional host display metadata. Requires source images and an image inspection/editing tool.

## Architectural cinematic maquette

**Task:** Make an architectural model readable as a browser-based spatial presentation.

**Operation:** Inspect the actual model; preserve material families through export; compose camera chapters; test the relationship between the scene, narrative and interaction; retain useful corrections.

**Project evidence:** In Resort Residence, a view facing a bench and glazing failed to communicate entry. Model inquiry and a revised camera revealed the lounge through the clear opening. The correction record also preserves material-export and interaction lessons; the method was adapted to Pin-Point.

**Status:** Applied browser workflow with project-specific visual and interaction review.

**Package:** [SKILL.md](skills/architectural-cinematic-maquette/SKILL.md) and its correction record. Requires project geometry, Blender or equivalent preparation, Three.js or equivalent browser implementation, and browser inspection.

## Interior rendering physics

**Task:** Translate an intended interior feeling into controllable image decisions.

**Operation:** Separate source, representation, attention, light, material and atmosphere passes. Compile the approved decisions before rendering. Audit the candidate, then propose a limited correction while freezing resolved variables.

**Evidence:** A developed state-based method with explicit human decisions and an inspectable calibration protocol.

**Status:** Experimental v0.1. The portable file is self-contained; it needs a supplied image and an image-editing tool when rendering is requested.

**Package:** [SKILL.md](skills/vaizel-interior-rendering-physics/SKILL.md).
